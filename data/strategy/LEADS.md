## 4. The priority order

Researcher's own, explicitly *for further production research* — not a
market-wide popularity ranking:

1. **Cosy everyday history** — homes, occupations, food, journeys, nighttime routines
2. **Difficult subjects explained gently** — maths/science with a distinctive narrator
3. **Space & natural-world curiosity** — strong demand, but **generic imitation has weak examples** (see §2)
4. **Emotional comfort / philosophy** — strong response, but *care on accuracy,
   therapeutic claims and monetisation durability* (Jung at Night)
5. **Specific game-world lore** — attributable rapid-monetisation case, but
   *historical earnings claim stronger than the currently visible catalogue*

> **The formula:** *a specific reason to click → a recognisable
> narrator/world → worthwhile material delivered comfortably → enough duration
> to remain useful after the viewer stops actively watching.*

**Two corrections to hustle advice:**
- **Length creates capacity for watch time, not demand.**
- **A breakout does not prove durable monetisation.**

## 5. Our two new ideas

### 5a. 🟢 "Silly level of advanced math" — Navier–Stokes, sleepy

**This is priority #2, and it's the safest niche we've found.**

- Maps directly onto **Bub Explains**: calculus 1.98M, algebra 1.43M, both in
  the first 68 days. **The lane is proven by a named channel.**
- **Rights: the cleanest of anything proposed.** Mathematics isn't copyrightable
  as expression; equations are public domain by nature. Science is fact. **No
  translation, no estate, no Path Press.**
- **Fits `DNA.md`'s enumeration skeleton** — one concept per unit, constant
  spacing, no climax. The 100-chapter philosophy video at 5.5M views is the
  shape.
- **Serves two audiences** exactly as Bub does: sleepers *and* people who want
  intimidation removed.

**Seed topics:** Navier–Stokes (Millennium Prize — turbulence, unsolved) ·
Riemann hypothesis · P vs NP · turbulence · black holes · entropy · Hilbert's
problems · Gödel · the three-body problem · shock waves ·
**silly-tier** — ordinals, large cardinals, monster groups, Moonshine.

**Sourcing risk: zero.** This is the one idea on the list with no rights
thread at all.

### 5b. 🟡 "Sad people's stories / everyday truth, each day is someone's story"

**The instinct is right. The framing as pitched is not.**

**Problem 1 — sensitive topic.** Framed as *depressed people's stories* this is
**health/mental-health content**, which is the **third inauthentic-content
category** (`RESEARCH.md` §2: *"AI personas used to discuss sensitive topics,
like health"*). Jung at Night's demonetisation is a live demonstration of the
risk in this exact lane.

**Reframe that keeps the emotional core:** *"each day is someone's story"* is
**narrative**, not **clinical**. Every problem in §3 (Oliver's ordinary homes,
Jung's companionship) can be served by *a person's life* without ever naming a
diagnosis.

### Sourcing — four options, ranked

| Source | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Public-domain diaries, letters, memoirs** | ✅ **best** | Civil War letters, Victorian diaries, emigrant correspondence, WWI trench letters, ship logs. **Enormous corpus, genuinely PD, genuinely "someone's real story"** — warm and sad without touching medicine |
| **Historical oral histories / archives** | ✅ clean | Library of Congress, national archives — check item-level rights (some PD, some restricted) |
| **Wikipedia biographical articles** | ⚠️ CC BY-SA | usable **with attribution + share-alike**; must credit |
| **Reddit / r/confession / r/depression** | 🔴 **no** | copyright held by author **plus** ToS on scraping **plus** it *is* the mental-health framing |
| News articles, StoryCorps, PostSecret | 🔴 | © |

**Recommended build:** *"The Diary"* — one real person's night, drawn from a
PD diary or letter collection, **read slowly**. Same emotional companionship
Jung at Night found, sourced entirely from the public domain, zero health
claims, and it slots straight into Oliver's **cosy-escape** lane.

> **Note on my own confidence:** I have **not** verified the size of the PD
> diary corpus with a search. Treat the ranking as reasoning from copyright
> status, not from measured volume. Worth a sourcing pass before committing.

### 7e. 🟢 Lacan · Baudrillard · Deleuze · critical theory — **corrected**

**Previous draft graded the corpus 🔴. That conflated *the ideas* with *a
specific translation*, and the owner was right to push back.**

**The line is not "is it copyrighted."** Copyright doesn't reach ideas,
concepts, or principles (17 U.S.C. §102(b)), and §107 explicitly favours
criticism, comment and scholarship.

| Use | Status |
|---|---|
| **Discussing** the Symbolic / Imaginary / Real · simulacra · the fold · dialectic of enlightenment · écriture | 🟢 **completely fine** |
| **Reading a modern English translation aloud**, verbatim, for hours | 🔴 reproduction of *that translator's* expression |

**Graded on demand — and the demand is the best in the file:**
`philosophy to sleep to` = **493,164 / 80% fresh / 12.0 views-per-sub.**

**And our architecture already lands on the right side:** powvid is
`facts.md` (our own claims + sources) → script in **our own words** → lint
gate. **That is discussion by construction.** `anakhya`'s discipline note
already says it — *"never present a translation as if it were the original."*

**What stays red after the correction:** reciting Tantrāloka, Dolores Canon or
Steiner's *modern English translations* aloud verbatim. Reproduction, full
stop — the use is the test, not the subject.

> **Net: "critical snooze" is unblocked.** The channel's format should be
> *analysis*, which is what we already build — and `ochema` remains the
> pre-1900 shortcut to the same register.
