ambient.*

## 1. The format

Not *"rain sounds for sleep"* — **"sleep in a place."**

A specific, contained, atmospheric scenario the listener *inhabits* for two
hours. The channel's job is to build the room, not to be quiet.

> **"people click for a particular curiosity or emotional need; 'for sleep'
> tells them how they can consume it"** — `LEADS.md`

The scenario **is** the curiosity. The sleep tag is the format.

## 3. ⚠️ A title trap the autocomplete exposes

`fall asleep in` is **dominated by a different intent:**

```
fall asleep in 3 minutes · in 2 minutes · in 60 seconds · in 5 minutes
```

**"Fall Asleep *in* a Mansion" competes in autocomplete with "fall asleep *in
3 minutes*"** — same stem, unrelated meaning. You'd be fighting a much larger
query for the same prefix.

### Use the stems that resolve cleanly

| ✅ Use | ❌ Avoid |
|---|---|
| **`Sleep in a [place]`** | `Fall asleep in a [place]` — collides with the *speed* intent |
| **`[Place] ambience for sleep`** | — |
| **`Night in a [place]`** | — |
| **`Rain in a [place] — sleep`** | — |
| **`[Place], [era] — to sleep to`** | — |

**`sleep in a ___` and `[place] ambience` are the two stems with no
competing intent.** Build the title grammar on those.

## 4. The archetypes

Every scenario must satisfy six criteria — otherwise it's a template:

| # | Criterion | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Contained** — a room, vehicle, vessel, cave | bounded = safe |
| 2 | **Protected** — something is *outside* and you're *inside* | the core comfort |
| 3 | **Sense-rich** — specific sounds, temperature, texture | gives the mind something soft to hold |
| 4 | **Passive** — nothing to do, nothing to decide | sleep precondition |
| 5 | **Time-anchored** — an era gives texture | Oliver's finding (`LEADS.md` §1) |
| 6 | **Soundscape generates itself** — rain, fire, wind, machinery | production is cheap |

### The archetypes that pass

| Archetype | Examples | Source notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Shelter-in-storm** 🥇 | cabin · tent · **bunker** · dugout · attic · ship's cabin | strongest comfort inversion |
| **Transit** | **sleeper train** · night flight · canal boat · ferry · RV | autofill-confirmed, 15 geo variants |
| **Enclosure / craft** | lighthouse · observatory · monastery cell · library back room | `lighthouse ambience` autofills |
| **Underground / depth** | cave · cellar · submarine · catacomb | `rain in a cave` autofills |
| **Elevated** | tower · treehouse · attic · crow's nest | |
| **Era / ordinary life** 🥇 | Victorian kitchen · medieval inn · 1950s diner at night · frontier cabin | **Oliver's proven lane** — *ordinary home 1700s* = 518K |
| **Fantastic** | hobbit hole · starship quarters · wizard's study · **magical forest** · **mansion** | `sleep in a spaceship` autofills twice |

## 5. Why this beats pure ambient — the psychological argument

Your earlier read: *"people want to shut their minds off."*

**A void doesn't do that.** An unstructured quiet room leaves the mind free to
scaffold its own anxieties — which is exactly what Sleep HushTV's commenters
complained about (*"discussing worries keeps reminding them of the worries"*
— `LEADS.md` §1).

> **A scenario gives the mind somewhere to *be* rather than nothing to think
> about.** It occupies attention just enough to crowd out everything else.

That's the mechanism behind Oliver's 518K ordinary-home video and Jung at
Night's 1M self-love video — **both give the listener a place to stand.**

**And it answers the Sleep HushTV failure directly:** don't name the anxiety,
**provide the room.**

## 7. The bunker case — handled honestly

**Demand is proven, not assumed.** We already measured it (`DNA.md` §3A):

| Video | Views | Age |
|---|---|---|
| *3+ Hours Of WW2 Facts To Fall Asleep To* | **11,843,332** | ~4 yrs |
| *3 Hours Of WW2 Facts To Fall Asleep To* | **9,520,499** | ~4 yrs |

**WWII-as-sleep is one of the biggest measured categories in our data.** And
those two videos are also our `quarterly` skeleton exemplar.

**But the scenario framing needs the right emphasis.**

| Framing | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **"Safe, dry, warm, underground — the war is somewhere else"** | ✅ **this is the product.** Shelter is one of the most basic comfort archetypes |
| Naming a specific regime in the *title* | ⚠️ **risk without sleep value.** Adds the off-putting axis for zero gain |
| Anything that lingers on victims or atrocity | 🔴 **`RESEARCH.md` §2 category 2: "off-putting or distressing content"** |

**The bunker sells shelter, not war.** The rain outside, the lamp, the cot, the
distant rumble, the fact that *you are fine*. Drop the regime from the title,
keep the architecture — and the video gets *safer* and *better*.

**Same rule for the other dark autofills** (`night in a graveyard`,
`night in a haunted house`): they work because the listener is warm and the
scary thing is **outside the glass**. Never move it inside.

## 11. *"Scientific focus music"* + why the music ones win

> *"im liking these music ones the most to be honest **because people just
> leave it on**"*

**That's the right instinct and it's measurable.** `RPM.md` §2: watch time per
view is the dominant revenue lever — **6 min = $2.68 RPM, 49 min = $18.00.**
Music maximises it precisely *because there's no narration to break the state.*

### The tradeoff, stated once

| | Narrated sleep | **Pure music/ambient** |
|---|---|---|
| Watch time per view | very high | **highest — nothing interrupts** |
| Published RPM band | $8–15 (dev.to) · $10.92 (OutlierKit) | **$0.80–2.50** (videopublishkit) |
| Production | script + TTS + mix | **loop + scene** |
| Template risk | low (text differs) | **higher — this is the flagged shape** |

Owner has said RPM is not the priority. **Fair — but note the third row:**
pure ambient is the format most exposed to `RESEARCH.md` §3's *"template with
little to no variation."* `SCENARIOS.md` §6 is what protects it: **every video
is a different place, so the visual and the soundscape both differ.**

### "Scientific focus music" — two readings, pick deliberately

| Reading | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Focus/study ambient** (a different time of day, same production) | 🟢 **clean.** Shares the *entire* pipeline with sleep; only the audience and the title change. Bub Explains' study-use commenters already prove the demand |
| **Binaural / frequency-based "scientifically tuned" claims** | ⚠️ **health-adjacent.** "Focus from 40Hz" starts drifting toward claims about cognition — `RESEARCH.md` §2 sensitive-topics territory |

**Recommendation:** build it as **focus/study ambience in specific places** —
*"Rain in a 1950s library, for study"* — and let the science live in *which
places and sounds*, never in *claims about the listener's brain.*
