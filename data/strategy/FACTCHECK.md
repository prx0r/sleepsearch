## FC-1 · *"$520,000/month combined, all under 90 days old"*
**Claimed:** @wannercashcow, relayed by @egoo_58's research dump · 2026-09-26
**Verdict: ❌ not supported by any measured figure. Off by ≥20×.**

### The arithmetic

| Assumption | Views required for **$520,000/month** |
|---|---|
| **$3.15 RPM** (only *sourced* creator figure we have — AdSense screenshot) | **165,079,365** |
| $6 RPM (planning floor) | 86,666,667 |
| **$10.92 RPM** (best published case, OutlierKit Jun 2026) | **47,619,048** |

Spread across channel counts, at best-case RPM:

| Channels | Views **per channel** per month | per day |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 9,523,810 | **317,460** |
| 10 | 4,761,905 | **158,730** |
| 30 | 1,587,302 | **52,910** |

### Against what we measured

**85 queries, 196 videos sampled.** The single highest-earning video in the
entire sample — a **378M-view, 3,068-day-old** white-noise upload — is worth
**$40,438/month at best-case RPM.**

> **$520k needs ~13 videos, EACH performing like the best of 85 queries.**

### And the killer: only 7 of 196 sampled videos are ≤90 days old

| Age | Views/day | $/mo @ best case | Query |
|---|---|---|---|
| 61d | **43,612** | **$14,287** | **history to sleep to** |
| 23d | 15,100 | $4,947 | sacred geometry to sleep to |
| 12d | 7,329 | $2,401 | occult to sleep to |
| 68d | 4,492 | $1,472 | mythology to sleep to |
| 54d | 3,304 | $1,082 | elder scrolls lore to sleep to |

**All seven, at their current rate, combined: $25,535/month.**

**520,000 / 25,535 ≈ 20×**

**The claim requires 20× the best young performers we found across 85 queries.**

### Corroboration

- **Of 139 top-ranked sleep channels we pulled metadata for, ZERO were created
  under 120 days ago.**
- Largest: **Soothing Relaxation — 5,070,853,327 views · 12.1M subs · 603
  videos · since Nov 2014.** That's **eleven years, not ninety days.**
- Sleep revenue **compounds on an 8–13 year tail** (`RESEARCH.md` §2.1,
  `GUIDES.md` §2.3). A 90-day channel has no tail — it has its first uploads.

### What *is* true in it

1. **Testing channels fast and concentrating on winners is a sound operating
   model** (`ORGANISE.md` §9's wave structure says the same thing without the
   spending).
2. **Young sleep videos *can* move** — see the 61-day-old `history to sleep to`
   above. That's our Phase 1 primary, and it's live.
3. **A 90-day channel *can* clear YPP** — sleep hours come free, that's
   `RESEARCH.md` §7's whole point.

### What isn't

The dollar figure. Nobody in our sample is within an order of magnitude of it,
and the structural reason is that **sleep pays on catalogue age, not upload
velocity.**

> **Rule:** a claim of monthly revenue needs a views figure and an RPM figure
> attached before it's admissible. *"Over $520,000"* with neither is a number
> designed to be repeated, not checked.

## FC-4 · The hook template — *"specific date → one defining detail → open loop"*
**Claimed:** pasted thread, Tarleton/Cowpens example · 2026-09-26
**Verdict: ⚠️ structure transfers, content must invert. One unsourced number.**

### What it gets right

| Element | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **No preamble** — story starts at full velocity, no "welcome to the channel" | ✅ **transfers directly.** Sleep scripts don't need a greeting either |
| **One defining detail per character**, never two | ✅ pacing advice, genre-neutral |
| **Specific beats generic** for establishing reality | ✅ matches `CATEGORIES.md` §6.2 (exact-match keywords ≠ ranking, but concrete beats vague) |
| *"first 30 seconds decide"* | ✅ MilX 2026: *"the hook: the first 7 to 30 seconds decide most of it"* |

### What doesn't

**The template is built on an OPEN LOOP.** *"What he rode into instead was one
of the most perfectly executed tactical traps..."* — the viewer stays to
discover **what happens next.**

**That is the exact opposite of sleep.** `DNA.md` measured it:

| | Story content | Sleep content |
|---|---|---|
| Retention driver | *want to know what happens* | **nothing should happen next** |
| Chapter labels | 93% neutral in sleep — **4% tension** | flat by design |
| Loop | **open** it | **close** it |

An open loop at 11pm makes you *keep watching*. Our product is *stop watching
by choice*. **Open a loop and you've built a competitor to your own video.**

### The unsourced number

> *"Below **70% retention in the first 30 seconds** and the algorithm
> deprioritizes the video regardless..."*

**YouTube publishes no retention threshold.** `SCHEDULE.md` already caught the
same thread-adjacent ecosystem inventing precise numbers. Retention matters —
but 70% is a **rhetorical specific**, not a documented gate.

### What to actually take

> **Invert the template:**
> ```
> ON [specific time of night] / [a specific quiet place],
> [one concrete detail], [no disruption], [no expectation to meet],
> [and nothing will be asked of you for the next two hours].
> ```
> Hook = **permission to stop.** Not curiosity.

## FC-5 · The multi-channel / "bill payers" thread
**Claimed:** *"15–20 channels, cross six figures"* · 2026-09-26
**Verdict: ⚠️ two genuinely good ideas, one I got partly wrong, earnings self-reported.**

### Where it contradicts me — and who's right

`CHANNELS.md` §6 concluded: **"multiple weak channels lose to one well-run
one."** This thread says run volume first.

**They're reconcilable, and the thread says so itself:**

> *"**Run volume until you know which channels actually work, drop the rest**,
> and put those hours back into the titles and thumbnails on the ones that
> earn."*

That's `ORGANISE.md` §9's wave structure with a **consolidation step I had
under-specified.** The sequence isn't "one forever" — it's **broad → learn →
narrow.** Correcting `CHANNELS.md` accordingly rather than pretending the
tension doesn't exist.

### ✅ The idea worth stealing: attention is the fixed cost

> *"The work of managing a channel making **$15 a day** is identical to the
> work of managing one making **$1,000**. You still upload on schedule, still
> sign off every thumbnail, still check for policy strikes in the morning."*

**Marginal cost of a channel is ~constant regardless of its revenue.** That's a
better framing than anything in our docs — it explains *why* low performers
should be dropped rather than optimised: **they consume the same attention as
the earners and return 1/66th.**

> Self-reported: a member went 15 channels @ $5–6K/mo → **7 channels @
> $15–20K/mo.** Cut in half, tripled. **Claim, not audit.**

### ✅ The second idea: give every channel a job

> *"This one covers rent. This one is the investment channel. This one covers
> the tools. This one is the experiment."*
>
> *"**Naming what a channel is for stops you making emotional decisions about
> it** — and emotional decisions about assets are what end people."*

**Adding to `ORGANISE.md`.** Cheap, and it's a *decision hygiene* mechanism,
not a strategy — which is why it works at every scale.

### ⚠️ Seasonal dip — real, and different from ours

> *"Mine went from **$1,500 a day to $300 a day when summer arrived** and
> nothing was wrong with it. The audience was just out enjoying their summer."*

That's **audience absence**, not advertiser rates. `RPM.md` §4 covers the
mirror-image **Q1 −30–50%** which *is* advertiser behaviour.

**Two different dips, both normal.** Pre-committed in `RPM.md` for Q1; now
noting **Q2–Q3 summer** separately so a July drop doesn't read as failure.

## FC-6 · Ravelin — *"title for reach, specifics inside"*
**Claimed:** 24.9K subs · 33 videos · one at 1.2M · 2026-09-26
**Verdict: ✅ the title principle transfers. ❌ the thumbnail verbs don't. Two unsourced numbers.**

### The comparison

| Title | Views |
|---|---|
| *"The Genius Trap Every Military Strategist Knows"* | **1.2M** |
| *"The Day Britain Lost an Entire Army, Saratoga, 1777"* | **265K** |

> *"Remove every detail limiting your audience. **Keep the curiosity. Kill the
> specificity.** Let the specifics live inside the video where the viewer is
> already committed."*

**✅ This transfers cleanly to sleep.**

| Instead of | Try |
|---|---|
| `Navier–Stokes explained slowly to fall asleep to` | **The Equation That Still Has No Solution** |
| `History of Byzantine farming to sleep to` | **How the Roman Empire Fed Itself** |
| `Alchemical texts to sleep to` | **The Recipe Nobody Has Been Able to Reproduce** |

Topic narrows the audience; **the promise doesn't have to.**

### ❌ The thumbnail verbs don't transfer

The pattern is `FOOLED · TRICKED · CRUSHED` — *"every verb signals dominance
and deception."*

**High-arousal verbs on a sleep thumbnail are a genre violation.** The
principle transfers; the vocabulary doesn't.

> **Sleep's thumbnail promise is the inverse:** not *"you'll feel the
> satisfaction of watching the high-leverage get outplayed"* but
> ***"you're allowed to stop now."***

Same mechanism — **thumbnail text is a promise of how the viewer will feel** —
delivered in the opposite register.

### Unsourced numbers

- *"**Satisfaction gets 4X more clicks than education**"* — no source given
- **n=2** on the title comparison — same channel, two videos, one production
  standard. Suggestive of causation, **not proof of it** (thumbnails, publish
  timing and topic all differed too)

**Take the mechanism. Don't take the multiplier.**
