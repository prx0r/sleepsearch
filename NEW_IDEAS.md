## CATEGORY A: TECHNICAL REFERENCE (Effort: 2)

### 1. Sleepy Standards
**Pitch:** ISO and ASTM standards read slowly — the ultimate boring content
**Source:** ISO.org, ASTM.org, NIST
**Why it works:** Technical standards are comically boring. Numbers, measurements, specifications. Nobody reads them for fun. But people fall asleep to them.
**Example:** "ISO 8601. Date and time formats. Year: four digits. Month: two digits. Day: two digits. Separator: hyphen."
**Episodes:** 10,000+ standards exist
**Voice:** V03 Lecturer

### 2. Sleepy Patent Reads
**Pitch:** Patent applications read slowly — inventions described in excruciating detail
**Source:** Google Patents (free API), USPTO
**Why it works:** Patent language is deliberately precise and boring. "A system and method for..."
**Example:** "Patent US20240123456. A system for autonomous vehicle navigation using multi-sensor fusion. Claim 1: The system comprises..."
**Episodes:** Millions of patents
**Voice:** V03 Lecturer

### 3. Sleepy Building Codes
**Pitch:** Building codes and regulations read slowly
**Source:** IBC (International Building Code), local codes
**Why it works:** Regulations are inherently boring. Numbers, measurements, compliance requirements.
**Example:** "Section 705.8.1.2. Fire resistance rating. The wall shall have a fire resistance rating of not less than 1 hour."
**Episodes:** Thousands of sections
**Voice:** V03 Lecturer

### 4. Sleepy Tax Codes
**Pitch:** Tax regulations read slowly — the ultimate sleep aid
**Source:** IRS publications, tax codes
**Why it works:** Tax code is universally boring. Nobody reads it voluntarily.
**Example:** "Section 162. Trade or business expenses. (a) There shall be allowed as a deduction all the ordinary and necessary expenses paid or incurred during the taxable year."
**Episodes:** Thousands of sections
**Voice:** V03 Lecturer
